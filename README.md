# ⚡ GridSense AI – EV Grid Intelligence & Infrastructure Planning System

## 🚀 Overview

GridSense AI is an AI-powered decision support system designed to help electricity distribution companies (such as BESCOM) efficiently plan EV charging infrastructure while maintaining grid stability.

The platform predicts EV charging demand, monitors transformer and feeder loads, identifies high-risk zones, and recommends optimal charging schedules and infrastructure placement. By combining artificial intelligence with grid analytics, GridSense AI supports the transition toward sustainable electric mobility.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

The rapid adoption of Electric Vehicles (EVs) is placing increasing pressure on existing power distribution networks. Unplanned charging can lead to transformer overload, voltage instability, and inefficient utilization of grid resources.

GridSense AI addresses these challenges by providing intelligent demand prediction, grid-aware infrastructure planning, and smart charging recommendations.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. Collect EV charging and grid load data.
2. Predict future charging demand using AI.
3. Calculate total grid load.
4. Detect overload and high-risk conditions.
5. Recommend optimal charging schedules.
6. Suggest suitable locations for new EV charging stations.
7. Display insights through an interactive dashboard.
